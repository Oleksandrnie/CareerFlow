How analytics works

CareerFlow calculates analytics from recorded structured events. Mark submitted resumes, interview results, offers and final outcomes for accurate statistics.

Activity cards and the trend use events that happened within the selected period. The funnel and conversion table use vacancies added within that period and inspect their full history.

Several interview rounds count as several activity events, but as one vacancy in the funnel. Legacy vacancies archived during the analytics migration are intentionally excluded.
